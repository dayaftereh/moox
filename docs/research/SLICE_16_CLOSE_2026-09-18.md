## Final product contract

Slice 16 closes with a deliberately bounded but honest New Game contract.

Supported and authoritative:

- Galaxy Size: Small, Medium, Large, Huge;
- Galaxy Age: Mineral Rich, Normal, Organic Rich;
- Difficulty: Easy, Normal, Hard, Very Hard, Impossible;
- Starting Technology: Pre-Warp and Average;
- local player preset race: Human or Klackon;
- deterministic built-in AI composition with Darlok and, for three-player starts, the alternate supported race;
- 1 or 2 opponents / 2 or 3 total players;
- explicit Seed with deterministic replayable New Game generation;
- server-owned Game ID;
- image-led selector HMI across desktop and 390 px mobile;
- full-width Players & Empires composition roster;
- save/export/import/restore and built-in-AI turn progression for the accepted matrix.

Catalog-visible but intentionally planned/locked:

- Advanced Starting Civilization;
- local-player Darlok and remaining preset-race breadth not yet mechanically complete;
- 3-7 opponents / 4-8 total players.

No planned option silently falls back to a supported one.

## Why Slice 16.7 closes without enabling Advanced

Gate-2 feasibility work proved that the architecture can carry Advanced, but full parity depends on mechanics that now have dedicated downstream owners:

- Slice 17: ship/component breadth relevant to final fleet semantics;
- Slice 20: Espionage/intelligence;
- Slice 21: Colony / Buildings / Planet Effects / Pollution fidelity;
- Slice 22: Preset Race Completion + Custom Race Designer;
- Slice 23: Full Advanced Starting Civilization parity.

Keeping Advanced planned avoids introducing partially inert starting Buildings, approximated planet balancing, guessed difficulty behavior or an arbitrary fleet interpretation.

Permanent Advanced evidence remains in:

- docs/research/SLICE_16_7_GATE1_ADVANCED_PARITY_AUDIT_2026-09-18.md;
- docs/research/SLICE_16_7_GATE2_ADVANCED_DEFERRAL_HANDOFF_2026-09-18.md.
